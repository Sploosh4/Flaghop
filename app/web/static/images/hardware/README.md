# Hardware artwork

The six original transparent pictures and their matching silhouette vectors are
the approved FlagHop Figma assets. Pictures were generated for this project;
their bytes are preserved from the approved device-artwork pack. The contours
are separate editable vectors, rendered at their native 70 × 70 size.

Grey indicates hover or keyboard focus; purple indicates selection. The artwork
does not establish device identity, vulnerability severity or a physical link.
